## Description
Todo is a fast, focused to-do app built for Apple devices.

• Smart lists: Today, Scheduled, All, Flagged and Completed, kept up to date automatically
• Your own lists: pick a color and icon for each one
• Due dates and times, with reminders when a task is due
• Notes, priority, flags and subtasks for bigger jobs
• Home Screen and desktop widgets: check off today's tasks without opening the app
• Private iCloud sync: your tasks stay in your own iCloud account, on all your devices
• Built for each device: a sidebar on iPad and Mac, keyboard shortcuts, dark mode and Dynamic Type

Sign in with Apple is optional. We don't collect any data.
